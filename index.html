Beginners guide for dodgeball



Basic Rules:
    1. Don't get hit
    2. Throw properly to hit the enemy in order to eliminate them
    3. Dodge every ball that enemies throws at you.
    4. You can use balls to block or deflect balls thrown at you.
    5. You can get back in the game when your teammate manages to eliminate an enemy by catching their ball succesfully.
Dodgeball Equipment:
    1. Sneakers
    2. Ball
    3. Elbow pads
    4. Knee pads
Basic Technique
    1. Throw straight
    2.Dodge properly
    3. Use your instincts well, and focus
